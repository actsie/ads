# Facebook Ad Hook Research — Beverage Shop Niche

## Hook Formats That Work (Ranked for Our Use Case)

### 1. Direct Address + Consequence
Strongest for cold audiences. Qualifies the viewer instantly.
`[Role], you're losing [specific $amount] every [time period] to [named problem].`

### 2. Curiosity Gap
Opens a loop the brain has to resolve.
`The reason [thing they currently do] keeps failing isn't what you think.`

### 3. Bold Stat First
Flash a specific, non-round number on screen in frame 1. Specific = credible.

### 4. Reverse Psychology
Feels like advice, not advertising.
`Stop [common habit]. Here's what it's actually costing you.`

### 5. Name the Workaround
Name the exact manual workaround they're doing (clipboard, spreadsheet, gut feel).
When they feel seen, they stop scrolling.

### 6. Reframe the Root Cause
Challenge something they already believe.
`You think it's a [supplier/staff/busy period] problem. It's not.`

## Pain Language — Their Actual Words

- "We run out and I don't know until someone tells me it's gone"
- "I'm ordering from memory and I know that's bad but I don't have time"
- "We always over-order beer and under-order wine — every single week"
- "I don't actually know what my pour cost is right now"
- "I work 70 hours a week and I'm still behind"
- "I live at this place"
- "I can't trust anyone else to do the ordering because they don't know what we need"
- "Food waste is killing us but I can't see exactly where it's happening"

**Deepest pain:** Being the only person who can hold things together,
combined with the fear that money is leaking somewhere they can't see.

## Facebook-Specific Rules

- **85% of Facebook videos are watched on mute** — text overlay must carry the full hook
- **First 0.5 seconds is a visual problem**, not a copy problem
- **Creative fatigue:** ~37% performance drop after 7 days — need 3+ hook variations per campaign
- **Hook rate benchmarks:** 25-30% = baseline, 30-45% = top performer
- **Facebook allows 2-3 second build** before payoff (slower than TikTok)
- **High-performing hooks show specificity** — "$800/week" beats "wasted money"

## Best Hook Angles for Inventory/Ops Problem Ads

### Angle 1 — Make the invisible cost visible
Don't say "improve your inventory management."
Say "You're losing $X a week and you're calling it just how it is."

Example:
> "Your bar is losing money every week. Your supplier isn't the problem."

### Angle 2 — Name the workaround
Every operator has one — a spreadsheet, a whiteboard, a walk-and-count ritual.
Name it and they feel seen.

Example:
> "Still walking your fridge with a clipboard every Monday morning?"
> "Most bars reorder the same way they did 10 years ago — by walking around and guessing."

### Angle 3 — Reframe the root cause
Operators frequently misdiagnose their problems. Reframing delivers a genuine insight.

Example:
> "You think you're running out of stock because your supplier is unreliable. Usually it's something else."
> "Your bar is losing money every week. Your supplier isn't the problem."

### Angle 4 — Social proof with specificity (warm/retargeting only)
> "We helped 47 independent bars cut their weekly ordering time from 3 hours to 20 minutes."

## Copy: Good vs Bad

| Bad | Why | Good |
|---|---|---|
| "Streamline your inventory operations" | Generic, no one talks like this | "Still counting stock by hand?" |
| "Optimize your reorder workflow" | Software pitch language | "Most bars reorder on gut feel — and it costs them" |
| "The clipboard workaround is costing you" | Jargon, not how they think | "Still walking your fridge before every order?" |
| "Get visibility into your operations" | Vague, sounds like a pitch | "Your best bartender leaving takes your whole inventory system with them" |
| Made-up round numbers ("$5,000/month") | Feels invented, loses credibility | Specific non-round numbers or their own words |
